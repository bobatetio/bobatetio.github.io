The way in

Both marketing pages hand off to the app through these. Signing up lands you in the app with onboarding waiting; signing in goes straight to the dashboard.

  • Sign up as a hotel → /app/, onboarding gate opens
  • Sign up as a creator → /creator/, onboarding gate opens
  • Sign in, hotel → /app/
  • Sign in, creator → /creator/

Everything else

/signin/ is the shared door back in after signing out of either app; it remembers which side you were on. /philosophy-demo/ is a motion prototype. /terms/ is the terms page. Onboarding happens inside the app, not on a page of its own. See the README for how the two halves are laid out.
